MONUMENT ATLAS / FIELD NOTES

A monument in layers.

Compare the present-day ruins with a plausible ancient reconstruction. Both share an approximate 189 × 156 metre footprint and a 48 metre outer wall. The ancient form does not represent one precise historical date.

Evidence and interpretation

The surviving exterior guides the complete arcades and attic. The archaeological park documents marble seating, red-and-white plaster in passages, and an arena floor above the underground machinery. Exhibition models inform the seating bowl and upper colonnade.

The exact seat count, stair and ramp routes, timber upper seats, mast dimensions, colors and decorative profiles are simplified interpretations. The awning fabric is shown stowed. Statues, painted figures, all underground rooms, and the surrounding city are outside this study. In the ruins, the arena platform and visitor routes remain approximate.
